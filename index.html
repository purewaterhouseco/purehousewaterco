Signs your water needs attention

Most homes deal with at least one of these — and most homeowners assume it's normal.

Crusty fixtures & spotty dishes

Hard-water minerals leave white scale on faucets, glass shower doors and every dish that comes out of the dishwasher.

Dry skin & dull hair

Chlorine and dissolved minerals strip natural oils, so showers leave skin tight and hair harder to manage.

Off taste & odor

A pool-like smell, metallic aftertaste or earthy flavor is a sign something in the supply needs to be treated.

Shorter appliance life

Scale builds up inside water heaters, coffee makers and washing machines, cutting efficiency and lifespan.

FAQ

Common questions

Is the water test really free?

Yes. A technician comes to your home, tests your tap water on the spot, explains the results in plain language and leaves you a written recommendation. There's no charge and no obligation.

How long does installation take?

Most whole-home systems are installed in about half a day. Under-sink drinking water systems usually take one to two hours. We clean up and walk you through the system before we leave.

What's the difference between a softener and a filter?

A softener removes hardness minerals (calcium and magnesium) that cause scale. A filter removes things like chlorine, chloramine, sediment and odors. Both of our packages handle softening and filtration.

How much maintenance is involved?

Softeners need salt added every few weeks, and filters are replaced on a schedule based on your usage. We can set up reminders or handle it for you with a maintenance plan.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — we offer monthly payment options with approved credit. Ask your technician for details during your free test.

What warranty comes with the system?

Every system includes manufacturer coverage on tanks and valves plus our own workmanship guarantee on installation. Your technician will give you the exact terms in writing.
